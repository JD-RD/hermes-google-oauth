Hermes Google Workspace

A personal, single-user OAuth integration.

This application is an OAuth client used by one person to let a self-hosted assistant (Hermes Agent, running on a private machine) read and edit that same person's own Google Calendar, Google Drive and Google Docs.

It is not a product or a public service. There are no other users, no accounts, no sign-up, and no data collection beyond the Google APIs the owner explicitly authorises.
